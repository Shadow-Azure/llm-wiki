# China 5G-A / ISAC / compute-network context

## Notes

- This is a secondary-media source used only as context for China's data/compute infrastructure; it is not a direct AI-RAN Alliance architecture source.
- China reportedly has 5G-Advanced infrastructure across more than **330 cities**, under 3GPP Release 18.
- MIIT has dedicated U6GHz (6,425–7,125 MHz) for 6G testing; the article argues this creates propagation, capacity and interference datasets relevant to standards work.
- China Mobile is reported to cut 2026 total capex by **9.5%** to about **RMB 136.6B** while increasing computing-power-network investment by **62.4%** to **RMB 37.8B**.
- China Telecom is reported to reduce total capex by **9.2%** while directing **35%** of remaining budget to computing infrastructure.
- China Mobile is reported to lead multiple 6G projects in 3GPP and to serve as lead rapporteur for the first formal 6G scenario/demand standardization project.
- ISAC is presented as a shared-waveform/hardware sensing capability and was designated by the article as a “Day 1” 6G feature at 3GPP RAN #108 in June 2025.

## Relevance to AI-RAN

- China's large-scale 5G-A, U6GHz field data, ISAC trials and operator compute-network spending create a data/compute foundation adjacent to AI-RAN.
- However, this source does not show the same vendor-neutral AI-RAN stack as AI-RAN Alliance; the clearest direct Chinese AI-RAN evidence found in this pass is the China Mobile/ZTE RAN-native AI case cited by AvidThink and AsiaInfo's MWC 2026 real-time wireless link-adaptation demo.
