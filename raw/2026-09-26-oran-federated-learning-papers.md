# O-RAN Federated Learning arXiv Samples

## Sample 1: Communication and Computation Efficient Split Federated Learning in O-RAN

- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02534v1>
- Published: 2025-08-04.
- O-RAN's hierarchical architecture enables FL using data from Non-RT and Near-RT RICs.
- As models grow, training time can jeopardize Non-RT/Near-RT deadlines.
- Split federated learning offloads partial model layers from Near-RT RIC to higher-performance Non-RT RIC.
- The paper highlights frequent data/gradient transfer and communication cost as deployment challenges.

## Sample 2: Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for Resource Allocation in O-RAN Slicing

- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01736v1>
- Published: 2022-08-08.
- O-RAN provides an open environment; coordinating xApps is important for network performance.
- The paper proposes federated deep RL to coordinate independent xApps.
- It designs a power-control xApp and a slice-based resource-allocation xApp.

## Sample 3: Autonomous CSI Prediction Framework for O-RAN-Enabled 5G mmWave Vehicular Networks

- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21963v1>
- Published/accepted: 2026-07-24.
- gNBs collect and label datasets to train CSI prediction models independently and with federated learning.
- Dataset inputs combine CSI feedback and C-V2X cooperative awareness messages.
- The framework targets O-RAN ML/AI applications such as rApps and xApps, using real-world mobility/CSI from DeepMIMO.

## 阅读定位

这些论文说明联邦/梯度级数据不只是隐私口号：它要面对 Non-RT/Near-RT 时间尺度、通信开销、模型大小、xApp 协同、CSI/流量预测和跨站点非独立同分布等工程问题。
