# SoftBank AI-for-RAN verification

## Three verified use cases

1. **Uplink Channel Interpolation**
   - Motivation: 5G TDD has fewer UL slots, while multimodal GenAI traffic is expected to raise the UL/DL ratio.
   - Method: AI improves channel-estimation accuracy.
   - Result: about **20% UL user-throughput improvement** in poor network-quality areas in a lab test with smartphones.
2. **Sounding Reference Signals Prediction**
   - Motivation: as device count increases, SRS intervals lengthen; high speed increases channel fluctuation and makes prediction hard.
   - Method: Multilayer Perceptron predicts SRS during non-reception intervals in a system-level simulator.
   - Result: about **13% DL throughput improvement** for devices at 80 km/h.
3. **AI-Driven MAC Scheduling**
   - Method: MLP-based MAC scheduling/user pairing for MU-MIMO.
   - Result: about **8% average user-throughput improvement**.

## Platform / collaboration

- SoftBank built a system-level simulator as an AI-for-RAN verification platform.
- The announcement includes collaboration with NVIDIA and Fujitsu; Fujitsu contributed AI technology to uplink performance improvement.
- SoftBank emphasizes that capacity expansion was achieved without additional base stations, implying potential capex avoidance.
